This website is a project by Maxwell Vo for his class project in ISTM 209. This website covers two Texas A&M traditions: Silver Taps and Fish Walk. Silver Taps is an official Aggie tradition while Fish Walk is a made up one. The page we are currently on is the home page, which has an overview of what this website is about and options to navigate for information. There are two other pages: Traditions and Members. Traditions holds more information about the traditions outlined on the website, while the Members Page introduces you to the group that created this page. Here, there's only one group member: Maxwell Vo. Around these pages are various HTML features with the intent of showcasing proof that various skills have been required from this course. While the site isn't comprehensive in all its information nor clarity, it serves its purpose of introducing an old aggie tradition with sufficient paths to further reading and also introducing theoretical tradition.
